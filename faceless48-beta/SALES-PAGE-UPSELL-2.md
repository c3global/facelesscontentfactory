**1. Talking-head edits.** Vertical or horizontal clips made from your own footage, with captions, timed graphics, and cutaways.

**8. A storyboard and a built-in checker.** See your whole video as numbered frames, in order, with the words under each one, and approve it before anything renders. The checker flags captions that cover a graphic, captions that repeat what is already on screen, and effects that land on the wrong word.

**9. Music mixing.** Your music sits under your voice and fades out cleanly at the end.

Plus batch rendering, to make several videos in one go.

## 12. STRAIGHT TALK ABOUT THE BETA

This is a real tool you run on your own computer. It is not a browser app.

- It needs Claude Code or Codex, with access to your project folders.
- It needs a short one-time setup. The built-in check tells you what to install.
- Rendering happens on your computer, so speed depends on your machine.
- Automatic captions use an extra tool that you install. The cutout that places you in front of graphics needs another tool and a large one-time download, and it takes a while on an ordinary computer.
- It does not include hosted rendering, automatic social posting, or AI video generation.
- Not every effect combination will look right the first time. That's why you preview and ask for changes.

Beta members shape what gets fixed and built next. Our next builds are more caption styles and a way to hand Claude a reel you love so it builds your look from it.

Mac or Windows.

Usually 30 minutes to an hour. Longer videos take longer.

You aren't sitting there watching it. It runs in the background while you film the next one, answer emails, or take a walk.

**Is it just for reels?**

No. YouTube videos too.
