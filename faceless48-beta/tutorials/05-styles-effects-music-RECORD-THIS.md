# Tutorial 5: Styles, effects and music. Your recording

**What to record:** your voice only, reading the text below. No screen, no camera.
**Length:** about 508 words, roughly 3.4 minutes at a relaxed pace.

## How to record
- A quiet room, your normal speaking pace, one take you like. Mistakes are fine: say the line again and keep going. I cut the repeats.
- Where you see (short pause), take a breath. Do not read the words in parentheses.
- Leave about one second of silence before the first word and after the last.
- Save it as WAV, MP3 or M4A. Send it here in the chat. If it is too big, share a link and set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view", then back to Restricted when I confirm I have it.
- Change any word that does not sound like you. Tell me what you changed so the on-screen words match.

## What I need from you before I build
- **One licensed music track** (MP3 or WAV) that you own or have the license for. I will not pick or download music for you. It is used for the before and after demo.
- Your talking-head clip is not needed. I use a clip already in the project for the six-styles side by side.
- Nothing else. Record the voice and send it.

## What I build from your recording
The same short clip in all six styles plus Yap side by side, a tour of effects from the 111-effect library, and one finished video before and after your music is added.

## Read this

Hi, it's Dr. CK. You never have to start from a blank page. This video shows you how to choose a look, how effects work, and how to add music.

Let's start with styles. A style is a saved bundle of choices. Here is one clip in six styles.

Signature is my everyday look. Kinetic editorial captions and the metal trim from your brand. (short pause) Quiet Authority is calm and premium, with pill-style captions, a plain black background and soft music. Spotlight is dramatic. Heavy captions, velvet, a copper outline and big moments. Show and Tell is for teaching. Your points appear as you say them, with stacks, checklists and callouts. Hype Reel is fast and loud, with orbit and wall effects and full sound. And Raw Tape is unpolished and real, with pill captions, grain and no music.

There's a seventh, called Yap, for casual videos. It gets its own video.

How do you pick? Say it in chat. Make this one Spotlight. Done.

You can also mix. Say: Make this one Spotlight, but keep my Signature captions. Behind every style are separate dials: the captions, the background, the finish, the color look, the music level, the sound, the outline, and the effects. A style just sets all of them at once. You can change one without losing the rest.

Now effects. There are more than 100 in the library. Animated text and cards. Zooms and camera moves. Spotlights. Orbiting elements. Clones. Phone screens and checklist graphics. Charts. Transitions. End cards. (short pause) You do not pick them from a menu. Your assistant reads your script, finds the moments that deserve one, and places each effect on the exact word.

It follows a few rules. One strong effect every four to six seconds is plenty. No effect sits on your face. And it uses effects that add something instead of repeating the caption.

Some effects put you in front of the graphic, like a type wall behind you. Those need a cutout of you. The default cutout is quick. If the edge isn't clean, the assistant can use a higher-quality cutout. It needs a one-time download, and it takes longer, so it only does it for the seconds an effect needs. It asks before downloading anything big.

If an effect doesn't look right the first time, that's normal. You preview, you tell it what to change, and it adjusts.

Now music. You can use your own licensed music. Put your track in the music folder and say: Add my music. It lowers the music under your voice, it fades in and out, and it matches the length of your video. (short pause) Use only music you own or have the license for. I can't give you a track to redistribute, and I'd never tell you it's fine to use something you found. The assistant can't hear, so you approve the mix by ear.

So that's it. Pick a style, mix it if you like, let the assistant place the effects, and add your own music. I'll see you in the next video.
