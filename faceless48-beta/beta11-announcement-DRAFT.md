## Community post

**Title:** Tech Tuesday: Beta 11 is here!

Happy Tech Tuesday! Beta 11 is ready, and this one is all about making your talking-head videos look like they came from a real editor.

## 8 talking-head templates

You now have eight finished looks to choose from: Quiet Authority, Magazine, Glass, Scrapbook, Analyst, Cinematic, Creator Hype and Teach Board. Each one has its own captions, its own backdrop, its own quote card, its own frame around your video and its own signature effects. So your Monday video and your Thursday video don't have to look like the same video twice.

Just tell your assistant which one you want: "Make this in the Glass template."

Below is the same video made in all eight, so you can see how different they feel.

[8 template sample videos]

## Your layouts move with you

The templates switch your layout as you talk: full screen, a window with a card under it, a split screen, a circle, a full quote card. The eye never sits on one layout long enough to get bored.

## The Template Effect Map is in the plug-in

Pick the effects you like for each template, save them as your kits, and copy them straight to your assistant. It works right inside the plug-in, no internet needed.

## Cleaner edits, automatically

A new clean-cut step removes long pauses, retakes and stumbles before anything else happens. Captions appear word by word as you say them, and they never repeat words that are already on the screen.

## Two new effects for teaching

The lesson board writes your points on a whiteboard as you say them, and the page turn peels the corner of the screen to the next idea.

## More carousel templates

Video carousels now come with five ready-made templates, plus a background picture option.

## Still all yours

Every template, effect and carousel uses your brand: your colors, your fonts, your metal finish. What you see here is my brand. Yours will look like yours.

## How to get it

Open Lesson 1 in the plug-in course and download the beta.11 zip. Unzip it into a fresh folder, then say "set me up."

This is still a beta, so you're helping me shape it. If anything trips you up, email support@faceless48.com and tell me what you asked and what you saw. Please never send passwords or keys.

Post your first template video in Showcase. I want to see which one you pick!

Dr. CK
