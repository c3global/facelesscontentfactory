## What a video carousel is

A video carousel is a carousel that moves. You know the swipeable posts with one idea per slide? This is that, except every slide animates: headlines rise in, checklists tick off, graphs draw themselves, numbers count up, and handwritten notes write on with little arrows pointing at what matters.

You don't design anything. Tell your assistant your idea, or paste the text for each slide, and say "make a video carousel about..." Your assistant writes the slides with you, picks a layout for each one, and shows you a storyboard before anything renders.

You can post it two ways:

- **As a carousel post:** each slide becomes its own short video, and the music flows from one slide into the next as people swipe.
- **As one video:** the whole carousel plays as a Reel.

It's always in your brand: your colors, your fonts, your metal finish and your logo. You can also add yourself or your AI avatar to the slides from a photo.

## Meet Carousel Studio

Here's the new part. Until now, if you wanted a headline a little higher or a card tilted, you had to ask your assistant and wait for another preview. Now you can move it yourself.

Say "open Carousel Studio" and an editor opens in your browser, right on your computer. Pick a carousel, pick a slide, and:

- **Move anything:** drag the whole slide's content, or one piece of it, wherever you want it
- **Tilt and resize:** rotate any piece and make it bigger or smaller
- **Rewrite your headlines** and choose where each line wraps
- **Add an icon or a character:** starter icons come drawn in your brand color, and you can add your own
- **Stack the layers:** bring a piece to the front or send it behind something else

Undo and redo are built in. When you save, Carousel Studio saves a new version, so your original is never overwritten. When you're happy, tell your assistant to render it.

## Your six carousel templates

You don't have to start from a blank slide. Pick a template, and your assistant rewrites every slide in your words for your topic.

1. **Tech:** dark glass panels, an animated status panel with a checklist that ticks off, a branching plan, and a link-in-bio ending. Great for tools and processes.
2. **Influencer:** big, bold type on your brand color, a strong hook, a numbered list, a quote and a "save this" ending. No photo of you needed.
3. **Showcase:** warm and editorial, made to show off your clips, with handwritten notes and arrows, a graph, an A vs B comparison and a photo collage ending.
4. **Glass:** a deep night background with drifting colored light and frosted glass cards: a fan of pictures, a list of switches that turn on one by one, a proof card and a glowing graph.
5. **LinkedIn:** clean and light for professional audiences: a before-and-after, steps, a pull quote and a follow ending.
6. **Content Grid (new):** made for Carousel Studio. Your headline sits on a clean grid with icons, a character, a step-by-step card and a calendar card. Everything on it can be moved, tilted and layered.

Just say "use the Glass carousel template for my topic," or whichever one you like. And any carousel can switch to the Content Grid look inside Carousel Studio.

## New effects for your talking-head videos

Beta 12 also brings six new effects and fresh looks for seven you already have. Ask for any of them by name, and for the refreshed ones, ask for "the new look."

- **DM thread:** a direct message conversation types itself out on screen, perfect for "comment and I'll send it" offers
- **Review card:** stars pop in, then the review and the reviewer's name (real reviews only, word for word)
- **Highlighter:** a marker sweeps across your key words as you say them
- **Taped note:** a handwritten note taped right onto your video
- **Lower third:** your name and title, for introductions
- **Save tap:** "Save this for later," with the bookmark tapped and filled
- **Label-maker tape, new look:** the stack, sticky text and freeze sticker now print out as embossed label tape in your brand color
- **Instagram comments, new look:** comment bubble, comment drop and comment CTA now look like a real Instagram comment section, with the reply and your link underneath
- **Spotlight, new look:** a beam of light falls on you with dust drifting through it
- **Polaroid drop, new look:** up to four photos drop in and fan out
- **Phone mockup, new look:** a solid 3D phone that turns slowly and can show your own screen
