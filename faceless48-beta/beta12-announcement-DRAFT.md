## Community post

**Title:** Tech Tuesday: Beta 12 is here!

Happy Tech Tuesday! Beta 12 is ready, and this one puts you in the driver's seat on your carousels.

## Meet Carousel Studio

Until now, if you wanted the headline a little higher or the picture tilted, you had to ask your assistant and wait for another preview. Now you can just move it.

Say "open Carousel Studio" and a visual editor opens in your browser, right on your computer. Pick a carousel and a slide, then:

- Drag, rotate and resize the content, or any single piece of it
- Rewrite your headline and choose where it wraps
- Add an icon or a character, and move it anywhere
- Bring any piece to the front or send it to the back

Undo and redo are built in. Every time you save, Carousel Studio makes a new version, so your original is never overwritten.

[Carousel Studio screenshots]

## A sixth carousel template: Content Grid

Content Grid is made for Carousel Studio. Your headline sits on a clean grid with icons, a character, and workflow and calendar cards, all in your brand. Any of your other carousels can switch to the Content Grid look inside the studio with one button.

[Content Grid slides]

## Six new effects

- **DM thread:** a direct message conversation that types itself out, perfect for your "comment and I'll send it" offers
- **Review card:** stars pop in, then the review and the reviewer's name. Use real reviews only, word for word.
- **Highlighter:** a marker sweeps across your key words
- **Taped note:** a handwritten note taped to the screen
- **Lower third:** your name and title, for introductions
- **Save tap:** "Save this for later," with the bookmark tapped and filled

[New effects sheet]

## Upgrade batch 3

Add `"version": 2` (or just ask your assistant for the new look) on seven effects you already know:

- The stack, sticky text and freeze sticker now come in a label-maker look
- Comment bubble, comment drop and comment CTA share one Instagram comment look
- Spotlight now has a beam of light with dust drifting through it
- Polaroid drop fans out up to four photos
- Phone mockup is now a solid 3D phone that turns slowly and can show your own screen

The originals are still there. Nothing you already made changes.

## Still all yours

Every carousel, icon and effect uses your brand: your colors, your fonts, your metal finish. The starter icons are drawn in your brand color automatically. What you see here is a demo brand. Yours will look like yours.
