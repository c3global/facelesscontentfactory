Scene 11 / The honest part

Straight talk about the beta.

This is a real tool you run on your own computer. It is not a browser app.

  • It needs Claude Code or Codex, with access to your project folders.

  • It needs a short one-time setup. The built-in check tells you what to install.

  • Rendering happens on your computer, so speed depends on your machine.

  • Automatic captions use an extra tool that you install. The cutout that places you in front of graphics needs another tool and a large one-time download, and it takes a while on an ordinary computer.

  • It does not include hosted rendering, automatic social posting, or AI video generation.

  • Horizontal talking-head videos get captions, graphics, end cards, camera moves, and color looks. The rest of the effect library is vertical-only for now.

  • Not every effect combination will look right the first time. That's why you preview and ask for changes.

Yes. Your purchase includes the Faceless Content Collective, which opens November 1. Until then you're on the waitlist. The 9 walkthrough videos live there, along with a place to ask questions and see what other members make.

Does it work on my computer?

Mac or Windows. You also need a paid plan with Claude or ChatGPT, since the plug-in runs inside Claude Code or Codex. With Claude, the basic Pro plan is enough.

How long does an edit take?

Usually 30 minutes to an hour, longer for longer videos. It runs in the background while you film the next one, answer emails, or take a walk.

Is it just for reels?

No. It makes YouTube videos too, and horizontal works for talking-head videos and explainers.

Only if you want them to. Mix 7 talking-head looks with explainer styles, caption styles, and 100+ effects, then set your colors, logo, fonts, and a gold, rose gold, silver, or copper finish once. Yes. Use your own recorded clip, or build an explainer around a stick figure or your own character art.

The video software under the hood is free for individuals and very small companies. Larger companies need a license from its maker. The details are in the notices file inside the plug-in.
