# Life Scenes

Work in the member's project. Read `brand-profile.md` first. Then follow `docs/LIFE-SCENES.md` step by step; it is the full workflow and effect menu.

Non-negotiables (each one was a costly mistake before):
- Never change the color of the clips. They arrive already graded. No grades, glow, light leaks or letterbox bars unless the member asks.
- Never build on-screen text the member has not approved. Propose plain-English lines first and wait.
- Hard cuts only between clips. No fades, dissolves, tags, badges or end cards.
- If a voiceover is longer than the clip, play the clip at normal speed and hold the last frame (`holdFromSec`). Never slow the clip down.
- Never invent facts, numbers, quotes, clients or results.
- An assistant cannot hear. Say so, and let the member approve sound by ear.
