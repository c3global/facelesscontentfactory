# Host and scene choices

No host artwork is required for the default code-drawn stick woman. The shared rig animates limbs and expression without an image API. A customer can supply transparent PNG/WebP poses as scene.character instead.

Recommended optional host-art set: pointing, explaining, thinking, shrugging, front-neutral, hands-on-hips, waving, walking-in, thumbs-up, stop-hand and clasped hands. Keep canvas size, head scale and baseline consistent. The plugin does not infer pose variants from a single picture. The assistant selects supplied poses on scene changes or builds a custom pose schedule.

The five reusable layouts accept customer labels and transcript cues:
- Compare: paired ideas or alternatives.
- Numbered List: two to four ordered points.
- Layers: stacked concepts with progressive emphasis.
- Load versus Capacity: two supplied numeric quantities and a bar; not invented statistics.
- Process or Loop: ordered steps with direction/return markers.

Styles (whiteboard/editorial/dark) are independent of layouts and customer branding. For richer bespoke choreography, use the included rig and shared props. Historical episode source is a reference, not a guarantee that arbitrary voiceovers fit those episodes unchanged.
